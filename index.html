Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in economics at the Norwegian School of Economics (NHH).

I work on macroeconomics and macro-finance. My research asks how monetary and fiscal policy transmit through household portfolios, and how the distribution of wealth and behaviour across them shapes asset prices, risk premia, and aggregate demand.

Data and theory inform each other in my research: I explore the data for the underlying economic mechanisms and build analytical frameworks to understand them; the data in turn disciplines the macroeconomic models that produce quantitative implications.

I am currently based in St. Gallen, Switzerland. I am always happy to grab a coffee in St. Gallen or Zurich to discuss Economics, Finance, and other interesting topics!
